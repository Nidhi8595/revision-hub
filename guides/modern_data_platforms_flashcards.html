The one question that mattersNot "what can this platform do?" — every major platform can basically do everything now. The real question: "what does the platform manage for me, and what am I still responsible for designing and operating?"
Two phrases to keep straightOpen data ≠ open platform. Storage portability ≠ platform portability. Both pairs get mixed up constantly — and both distinctions are tested throughout this document.
